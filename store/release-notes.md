### For testers

**Chat**
- Swipe a message to the right to reply. Tap the quote in a reply to jump to the original.
- Long-press a message to react, reply or copy it. On a computer, hover over a message for the
  react and reply buttons.
- One reaction each per message. The sender is notified when you react.
- Send a photo (with an optional caption) from the image button, or a voice note from the mic
  (tap to start, tap send to finish). Voice notes play at 1×, 1.5× or 2×.
- Emoji skin tones: press and hold a hand or runner emoji, or tap ✋ in the emoji panel.
- Messages are grouped with day headings, and ticks show when your message was read.
- New "suggest a run" icon.

**Profile and discovery**
- Photos are no longer required. Pick an avatar to show instead.
- "Let runners find me" replaces "Seeing someone". Turn it off to hide from Discover and race
  runner lists; your matches, race chats and swaps carry on. New members see it at the end of
  sign-up.
- The widest search distance is now "Anywhere", with no limit.
- Light mode: Profile > Appearance. The app reloads to switch.

**Races**
- 130 more races for January to October 2027.
- Weekly parkruns, under the new "parkruns" view in the Races tab.
- See which runners from your clubs are going to a race.

**Notifications**
- A prompt asks you to turn on notifications if they're off, and explains how to unblock them if
  they're blocked.

**Please try and tell us**
- Send and play a voice note between an Android phone and the web app, both ways.
- Switch to light mode and look for any text that's hard to read.

**Known issues**
- Voice notes recorded in some web browsers can't be played on iPhones.
- "Match my phone" may stay dark until you install this update.
- A few text fields have a faint outline in light mode.
