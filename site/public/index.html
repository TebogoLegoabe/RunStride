Why RunStride

Dating apps don't know you run. RunStride does.

Your weekend already has a parkrun, a club run or a race in it. RunStride is built around that, not bolted onto it.

Matched on pace

Every profile carries a running match score built from pace, weekly distance, terrain, goals and the time of day you like to run.

Meet at races

See who else is running your next race, talk in its group chat, and find someone to share the start line with.

Swap race entries

Injured, or missed the entry? Find someone to swap with, then do the transfer on the organiser's official site.

Safe first runs

Suggest a run in the chat. On the day, share your live location with a trusted contact, check in, or press the panic button.

Private by design

Others see roughly how far away you are, never where you live. Your exact run location is deleted the moment you stop sharing.

Still useful when you're taken

Found someone? Switch dating off and keep the races, group chats and entry swaps. Your running life carries on.

Races

Every race is a place to meet.

South Africa's race calendar in one place, with the runners going to each one. Search by province, date or distance, and join the ones you're entered for.

  • Say you're going and pick your distance, or join as a supporter.
  • Talk to everyone going in the race chat, and @mention the people you've met.
  • Say hi privately to a runner, with a chat request they can accept or ignore.
  • Swipe only runners at that race when you want to meet before race day.

Entry swaps

Stop asking strangers in the comments.

Runners already trade entries in Instagram replies and WhatsApp groups, where nobody is verified and nobody is accountable. RunStride gives that a proper home, and keeps the transfer itself where it belongs: with the organiser.

Find someone

Post the entry you have, or set an alert for the distance you need. Sellers can see how many people want it.

Agree privately

Message each other in the app. Both of you are ID-verified, and either can report or block.

Transfer officially

Do the swap on the organiser's own site, so the right name and medical details are on the bib.
