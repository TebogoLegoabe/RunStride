Runners near you

See who runs nearby, without giving away where you live.

  • Approximate areas, never exact homes. Locations are rounded to about a kilometre before they're stored.
  • You choose how far. From your neighbourhood to the whole city.
  • Shared routes and races. See who runs your races, so you meet somewhere you both know.

First run together

Skip the coffee. Go for a run.

Suggest a run straight from the chat: a time, a public place and a distance. When they accept, it's in both your plans, with live sharing ready for the day.

  • Public places first. Promenades, parks and parkrun routes, not someone's front door.
  • Pace you both enjoy. Your running match tells you before you go.
  • Someone always knows. Share the run live with a friend, and check in when you're done.

Safety

Built for meeting a stranger outdoors.

  • Verified people. Everyone verifies their ID and a selfie before they can match.
  • Approximate distance only. Nobody sees where you live, just roughly how far away you are.
  • Live run sharing. A trusted contact can follow your run, and your exact location is deleted afterwards.
  • Panic button. One press texts your trusted contacts with where you are.
  • Report and block. A real person reviews every report.
  • Free for everyone. Safety features are never behind a paywall.
