Delete your account

Last updated: 14 October 2026

The short version

  • You can delete your account yourself, in the app, in under a minute.
  • Deleting removes your profile, photos, matches, chats and preferences straight away.
  • Can't get into the app? Email us and we'll delete it for you.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open RunStride and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

On the web at app.runstride.co.za the steps are the same.

If you can't sign in

Email privacy@runstride.co.za from the email address on your account, or tell us the phone number you signed up with, and say you want your account deleted. We may ask you to confirm it's you first. We'll delete it within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos, bio, running and dating preferences.
  • Your matches and the chats in them, your race attendance and your swap-board posts.
  • Your trusted contacts, run plans and any location shared during a run.
  • Any ID photos, selfie or ID number still waiting for review. (These are deleted as soon as a review is decided anyway.)
  • Your phone number, email address and sign-in links.

What we may keep, and why

  • Reports about you. If someone reported you, we keep the report and its evidence so that deleting an account can't erase proof of abuse. This is kept only as long as needed for safety and legal reasons.
  • A one-way fingerprint of your ID number, if you used the optional ID check. It can't be turned back into your number. It lets us stop a banned person coming back with a new account.
  • Backups. Deleted data can remain in our backups for a short period before they roll over.

See our privacy policy for the full picture, including your rights under POPIA.
