Child safety standards

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version

  • RunStride is for adults only. You must be 18 or older to use it.
  • We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Anyone can report a concern in the app, or by emailing support@runstride.co.za.
  • We remove offending accounts and report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

Who RunStride is for

RunStride is a dating and social app for adult runners in South Africa. Everyone must give their date of birth when they sign up, and the app refuses anyone under 18. Our terms of use forbid children from using RunStride and forbid anyone from using it to contact, groom or exploit a child.

What is not allowed

  • Any content that sexualises a child, including images, videos, drawings, AI-generated images and text.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a child, or with someone you believe to be a child, for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, trafficking, or any attempt to arrange contact with a child.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material anywhere on RunStride, including in chats.
  • Accounts that belong to, or pretend to be, someone under 18.

How we prevent it

  • Age check at sign-up. A date of birth is required and under-18s are refused.
  • Optional ID verification. People can verify with a South African ID, checked by a person, which also confirms their age.
  • Reporting in every profile and chat, with a dedicated "Seems under 18" reason.
  • Urgent review. An under-18 report alerts our team immediately. Accounts reported by several people are hidden from others while we review.
  • Evidence kept. When someone is reported, we keep a copy of the relevant profile and messages, so deleting an account can't erase proof.
  • Bans that stick. Banned people can't simply sign up again with the same ID number or phone number.

How to report a concern

  1. In the app, open the person's profile or your chat with them, tap Report and choose Seems under 18 (or the reason that fits best). Reporting someone also blocks them.
  2. Or email support@runstride.co.za with the person's name as shown in RunStride and what you saw. Please don't attach or forward any abusive images: describe them instead.

If a child is in immediate danger, call the SAPS on 10111 or Childline on 116 first.

What we do when we get a report

  • We review under-18 and child safety reports as a priority, aiming to act within 24 hours.
  • We remove content and ban accounts that break these standards.
  • We report child sexual abuse material and child exploitation to the South African Police Service, as South African law (including the Films and Publications Act and the Criminal Law (Sexual Offences and Related Matters) Amendment Act) requires, and to other relevant authorities such as NCMEC where appropriate.
  • We keep the evidence securely and share it only with the authorities.
