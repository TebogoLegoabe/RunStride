5. Your safety

RunStride checks identities, but we don't do criminal background checks and can't guarantee how anyone will behave. When you meet someone:

  • meet and run in busy, public places, in daylight where you can;
  • tell someone you trust, and use live run sharing and check-ins;
  • if you're in danger, call 10111 or 112 first. The panic button alerts your trusted contacts; it doesn't contact emergency services.

Running carries its own risks. Run within your fitness and get medical advice if you need it.

6. Races and entry swaps

Race information comes from organisers, public listings and users, and may be wrong or change. Always check with the organiser.

The swap board helps you find someone to swap a race entry with. Every transfer must be done through the organiser's official process, so the right person's name and medical details are on the bib. RunStride isn't part of any sale: we don't handle payments, and we aren't responsible for swaps between runners. Never pay until the organiser confirms the transfer, and report anyone who pressures you.
